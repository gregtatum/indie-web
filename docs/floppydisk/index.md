# Docs

Floppy Disk is a personal workspace built with indie web principles. Your creative documents and personal knowledge live on your own storage, not on someone else’s platform. Bring your writing, research, art, notes, and other creative work together in one place, accessible wherever you are.

## Indie web principles

- **Own your data.** Your work lives in ordinary files and folders that belong to you.
- **Stay portable.** Use standard formats like Markdown, PDF, and common image formats.
- **Skip the export.** Open, move, copy, or sync your files directly.
- **Choose your storage.** Keep your workspace locally, in Dropbox, on a NAS, or wherever works for you.

## What you can do

- Write and edit Markdown with a live preview.
- Bring together images, PDFs, and other reference material.
- Organize your work into projects and folders.
- Access your workspace across devices, with offline support.
