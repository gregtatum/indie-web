# Privacy Policy and Usage

{{SITE_DESCRIPTION}}

## Your files and data

Your files belong to you. Where they are stored depends on the storage option you choose.

### Browser storage

When you use browser storage, your files stay on your device and are stored by your browser. They are not uploaded or collected by this site. Keep in mind that clearing your browser data may also delete these files.

### Dropbox

When you connect Dropbox, access is limited to the app folder created for this site. The rest of your Dropbox is not accessible. Authorization is stored in your browser. Files and related information may also be cached locally as part of normal use, but are not shared outside of that.

Dropbox handles your files and account according to its own [privacy policy](https://dropbox.com/privacy).


### Local and network storage

You can connect folders from your computer, a home server, or a network storage device (NAS). Files in these folders remain on storage you control and are not copied elsewhere by this site.

The storage server only has access to the folders you choose to make available. When you open or edit files, their contents are transferred between your browser and that server as needed. The privacy and security of these files ultimately depend on how your storage server and network are configured.

## Hosting

The application is hosted by Netlify. Like most web hosts, Netlify may process information associated with requests to the site. See [Netlify's privacy policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/) for details.

## Analytics and usage data

This site does not use Google Analytics or similar third-party analytics services. I don't collect behavioral data for advertising, profiling, or tracking people across the web.

The services that keep the application running may collect basic operational information. For example, hosting providers such as Netlify or AWS may record requests, and Dropbox may keep track of API usage. These services handle that information according to their own privacy policies.

I may collect limited, privacy-conscious telemetry in the future to understand how the application is performing and which features are being used. If I do, the goal will be to collect only what's useful for maintaining and improving the project, without building profiles of individual users or tracking them across sites.

## Use at your own risk

This is a free, non-commercial personal project maintained by Greg Tatum. It is provided as-is, without guarantees about availability, reliability, or the integrity of your data.

Keep backups of anything important, especially when using browser storage or third-party storage services.

If you find a bug that could cause data loss or corruption, please [report it on GitHub](https://github.com/gregtatum/indie-web/).
