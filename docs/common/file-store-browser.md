# Browser

Browser storage is the simplest way to get started. Your workspace is stored directly in your browser, with no account, external storage, or setup required. It works well for trying things out, keeping a workspace on a single device, or working entirely offline.

Your files are stored using [IndexedDB](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Web/API/IndexedDB_API). They stay on this device and are only available from this browser. Clearing your browser data will also remove them.

For a workspace you want to access across devices or keep independently of your browser, choose another storage option such as Dropbox or a NAS.
