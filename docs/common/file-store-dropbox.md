# Dropbox

Dropbox is a good choice when you want your workspace to follow you across devices. Your files are stored in Dropbox and synced normally, so you can access them both from Floppy Disk and anywhere else you use Dropbox.

Connect your account at [Settings](/settings). Dropbox creates a private `Dropbox/Apps/{{SITE_DROPBOX_FOLDER}}` folder for your workspace and does not have access to the rest of your files.

Once connected, you can open, edit, and organize your workspace normally. Changes are saved back to Dropbox and synced across your devices.
