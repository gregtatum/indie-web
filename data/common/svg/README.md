## Brand lockups

`floppydisk-lockup.svg` (icon + wordmark) and `floppydisk-mark.svg` (icon
only) are generated by `art/build-floppydisk-lockup.py`, not hand-authored.
The disk shape comes from `art/floppy-disk.link.svg`; the wordmark is Hanken
Grotesk (the `--font-display` face) instanced at a display weight and
flattened to outlines so the SVG carries no font dependency. Rerun that
script after any copy or weight change.
